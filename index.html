Skill Unlocked

Advice: Should (+)

Use SHOULD to say that something is a good idea or to give advice.

"You should listen to me."

(It is a good idea to listen)

"He should be careful."

Notice: 'Should' is the same for everyone (I, You, He, She).

Skill Unlocked

Negative: Shouldn't (-)

Use SHOULDN'T to say that something is a bad idea.

"We shouldn't fight the Gods."

(It is a very bad idea)

"You shouldn't be angry."

Skill Unlocked

Questions (?)

To ask for advice, put SHOULD before the person.

"Should we help them?"

"What should I do now?"
